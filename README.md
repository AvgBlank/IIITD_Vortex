AI-Powered Credit Scoring & P2P Lending

Overview

An AI-powered credit scoring and peer-to-peer (P2P) lending platform that leverages alternative data to evaluate creditworthiness and facilitates secure, affordable micro-loans.

Features

AI-Based Credit Scoring: Leverages AI to assess creditworthiness.

Alternative Data Utilization: Uses non-traditional financial indicators for evaluation.

Transparent Lending: Ensures clear and unbiased loan approval processes.

Affordable Access: Offers fair interest rates for borrowers.

P2P Lending: Connects lenders directly with borrowers securely.

Benefits

Empowers the underbanked with financial opportunities.

Reduces bias in credit evaluation.

Enhances trust in the lending ecosystem.

Enables micro-loans with enhanced security and affordability.
